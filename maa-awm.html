What is the Purpose of the MAA/AWM Club?

The Mathematical Association of America (MAA) is a national organization dedicated to advancing the understanding of mathematics and its applications. It also has many opportunities for students to participate in research, competitions, and professional development.

The Association for Women in Mathematics (AWM), founded in 1971, encourages women to pursue careers in mathematics. At Mount St. Mary’s University, the AWM club creates a supportive and inclusive environment where students can explore mathematics and build confidence, even if they're not women. The president of the MAA/AWM club, Gabbie Stine, also noted that the club strives to make a positive impact on women in STEM and young students as they learn more about careers and topics in mathematics.

Who Oversees and Participates?

At Mount St. Mary’s University, these organizations are primarily supported by faculty in the Mathematics and Computer Science Department. The AWM club is led by an executive board, consisting of a president, vice president, treasurer, and secretary, serving as a Registered Student Organization with the Mount’s SGA.

Participation is open to all students, including mathematics majors, minors, and anyone with an interest in math, problem-solving, or analytical thinking.

How Can Students Join?

Students join MAA/AWM by attending meetings, signing up/participating in events, or reaching out to a faculty advisor or any active officers of the club. There are no strict, concrete requirements to join, so just having an interest in math and a willingness to participate makes the club accessible to everyone.

Club Meetings

Meetings happen about once a month, and they are not always math-centered. The AWM club has offered many events that include fun activities like trivia nights and movie nights, as seen on their Instagram page. At most gatherings, however, the MAA/AWM club focuses on volunteering or participating in math activities. Event dates are posted throughout the semester so all students have chances to attend, and the more involved you are the more opportunities there are to gain professional development and experience.

Notable Facts

  • AWM was founded in 1971 to support women in mathematics
  • The MAA has very interesting math blogs on their main website, which promote research, teaching, and student competitions
  • These clubs/organizations connect students with faculty and career opportunities
  • The MAA/AWM club also works in schools around the community and every spring semester they invite a professor from a nearby university to speak on a specialized math topic
  • The MAA/AWM club at MSMU is overseen by an amazing math professor and math department chair, Dr. Jonelle Hook

Why Join?

Joining the MAA/AWM club helps students expand on their mathematical knowledge, and it provides a safe community for women who share a passion for math to connect with each other. Also, it opens doors to internships, research, and networking opportunities through various means. No prior knowledge is needed, so there is no need to feel intimidated or hesitant to join or participate, so if you are willing to learn and share a love of math the MAA/AWM club is the best option for you!
